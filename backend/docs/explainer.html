1 · The big picture

The backend is the brain in the middle

The box at home (ESP32) measures electricity and water. The website shows them. The backend sits between the two: it receives each measurement, checks it, saves it in Supabase, does the math in dinars, and tells the website what to show.

2 · What I built

Five steps, in the order I built them

  1. 1

    The storage

    The database in Supabase: 10 tables for accounts, homes, devices, readings, alerts, tips, scores, badges and the STEG prices. Only the backend can open it; the public Supabase key sees nothing.

    models.py · supabase/migrations/
  2. 2

    The calculator

    Turns kWh into dinars with the STEG prices, guesses the end-of-month bill, and decides when to warn you. Prices are read from the database, so changing a price never needs new code.

    services/tariff.py · ingest.py · alerts.py · consumption.py
  3. 3

    The extras

    Tips that say how many dinars you save, a daily score with badges, the settings page, and the Simulate spike button for the jury.

    services/tips.py · score.py · demo.py · routers/
  4. 4

    The helpers

    A simulator that pretends to be the ESP32, a script that fills the database with a month of demo data, and a script that writes your .env file.

    simulator/simulator.py · scripts/seed_demo.py · scripts/setup_env.py
  5. 5

    The safety net

    71 automatic tests that check every rule, on a small test database and on real PostgreSQL with the same SQL as Supabase.

    tests/
3 · Where the data lives

Everything belongs to a home

A person has a home. The home has devices, and each device sends readings. Alerts, tips, scores and badges also belong to the home. The prices sit apart, so the team can change them like a spreadsheet.

4 · One reading, start to finish

Every measurement passes four checks

Each message from the box goes through the gates below, left to right. If one check fails, the message is refused with a code and never touches the bill. If all pass, it is saved and the alert rules run.

5 · Why the app exists

One extra kWh can cost 9 to 46 dinars

With the STEG prices in the specification, the price of the tranche you reach applies to every kWh of the month. So the bill does not grow smoothly: it jumps at 200, 300 and 500 kWh. The backend warns you inside the orange zone, 10 kWh before the jump.

6 · When the app warns you

Four kinds of alerts

Each alert is sent once, not again and again. For example, the 200 kWh warning comes once per month.

▲ Tranche alert

You are within 10 kWh of 200, 300 or 500 kWh, or the month's forecast goes past it.

"Encore 9 kWh avant la tranche suivante (200 kWh). La franchir coûterait environ 9,23 DT de plus (estimation)."
● Leak alert

Water keeps flowing without a single stop for 2 hours, between 01:00 and 05:00. The hours can be changed in Settings.

"Fuite probable : l'eau coule sans arrêt depuis 00:15 (3 h 15), environ 97 L utilisés."
■ Sensor fault

The box says a sensor did not answer. The app shows a notice instead of inventing a value.

"Le capteur eau (YF-S201) de l'appareil tg-001 ne répond pas."
○ Device offline

No message from the box for 5 minutes. It closes by itself when the box comes back.

"Appareil tg-001 hors ligne depuis 21/11 16:05. Vérifie le Wi-Fi et l'alimentation."
7 · The jury demo

What happens when you press "Simulate spike"

The demo month starts at about 104 kWh with no alert. One button press makes the alert and the tip appear in about 10 seconds.

8 · What you did on your laptop

Three steps done, one quick fix left

  • ✓ Done

    python -m scripts.setup_env

    Wrote your .env file: the database address with your password, plus two random secret keys.

  • ✓ Done

    python -m scripts.seed_demo …

    Created the demo and admin accounts, the two devices, and 2,000 readings of history in Supabase.

  • ✓ Done

    uvicorn app.main:app --reload

    Started the backend. It connected to Supabase, and the page localhost:8000/docs opened.

  • ! Fix

    python -m simulator.simulator

    The second terminal was not using the project's Python. Run .venv\Scripts\activate in it first (the prompt then starts with (.venv)), then start the simulator again.

9 · Still to check with the team

Four things to check

  • First tranche. The specification gives 176 millimes for 101 to 200 kWh, and nothing below 101. I made it start at 0 kWh.
  • Price rule. Does the tranche price apply to all kWh of the month? If not, one line in .env switches to the other rule (TARIFF_PRICING_MODE=progressive).
  • Water prices. The SONEDE grid is unknown, so water money and water tranche alerts stay off until someone adds the prices.
  • ESP32 key. The key of tg-001 was pasted in the chat. It can only send readings for that one device, but it can be replaced before you share anything.
