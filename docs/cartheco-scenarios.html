The home status

Five states, from best to worst

✓ okData fresh, forecast below the next threshold (A1, B1).
● attentionForecast will pass a threshold (B2), or this week is 25 % higher (B12).
▲ warningMonth total inside the alert margin, e.g. 190 / 200 kWh (B3).
■ criticalA threshold is crossed (B4), or a night leak (C4).
○ unavailableNo data: offline 30 s+ (E2), bad signal (D4), new home (F1).

The worst state wins: a leak makes the home "critical" even if electricity is fine. Acknowledging an alert ("Vu") takes it out of the count (F5).

One day in the Ben Salah home

A 6 kVA home near Tunis, late in the month at about 185 kWh. One day covers most of the system: appliances, the tranche, a sensor fault, a night leak and a power cut.

  1. 07:00okA4 · C1

    Breakfast

    Kettle (+2 100 W) and the tap at 4 L/min. Cartheco tags the jump as "Chauffe-eau électrique ou bouilloire" in the day's big consumers.

  2. 13:00attentionA3 · B2

    The AC runs all afternoon

    +1 350 W ("Climatiseur ou fer à repasser"). The month's forecast now passes 200 kWh.

    "Au rythme actuel, tu vas dépasser 200 kWh ce mois-ci. Passer cette tranche coûterait environ 9,74 DT de plus (estimation)."
  3. 19:00warningA6 · B3

    Dinner: oven, heater and water heater

    6.5 kW, above the 6 kVA subscribed (a real breaker could trip). The month enters the 10 kWh margin: 190 / 200 kWh.

    "Encore 10 kWh avant la tranche suivante (200 kWh). La franchir coûterait environ 9,74 DT de plus (estimation)."
  4. 21:00criticalB4

    One more hour of AC

    The month passes 200 kWh. Every kWh of the month is now priced at tranche 2, and the next kWh costs more.

    "Un seuil de tranche est franchi ce mois-ci."
  5. 22:00criticalD1

    The PZEM wire comes loose

    The ESP32 sends electricity: null. No value is invented: the power card says "capteur muet". Plugged back in 20 minutes, values return.

    "Le capteur électricité (PZEM-004T) de l'appareil ne répond pas. Vérifie son câblage."
  6. 01:00 → 03:00criticalC4

    A silent leak

    The toilet leaks 0.4 L/min and never stops. After 2 hours inside the 01:00–05:00 window: red alert, once for the night.

    "Fuite probable : l'eau coule sans arrêt depuis 01:00 (2 h 00), environ 48 L utilisés. Vérifie la chasse d'eau et les robinets."
  7. 04:00unavailableE2 · E3 · E4

    Power cut

    The ESP32 goes off too. After 30 s the data is "ancien" and the device offline; after 5 min a device-offline alert. Power back at 04:10: the alert closes by itself.

    "Appareil hors ligne depuis 04:00. Vérifie le Wi-Fi et l'alimentation."
  8. Next morningF5 · A9 · C4 tip

    Vu, and what to do

    The family taps "Vu" on the alerts, replaces the toilet flapper, and reads the tips with the dinars saved: fixing the leak, halving the standby load.

After the day: the next month

The forecast warns on day 10, the family stays under 200 kWh

New month. At 7.2 kWh a day, Cartheco forecasts 216 kWh on day 10 (B2) and suggests about 0.9 kWh less per day: water heater on a timer, standby off at night (A9, F7). The month closes at 198 kWh, still in tranche 1.

Small changes, visible dinars

The money is in the jumps: for a 6 kVA home, the 201st kWh adds 9.74 DT and the 301st adds 42.09 DT. Cartheco's job is to show the jump early enough to avoid it with small changes.

Families

  • A bill they can see and predict, in dinars, every day.
  • Leaks fixed the first night, not at the next quarterly SONEDE bill.
  • "Économique" homes (≤ 2 kVA) warned before losing their cheap bands.

STEG and the grid

  • Water heaters moved out of the evening peak: about 2 kW per home at the worst hour.
  • 1,000-home pilot: up to 2 MW less on the evening peak (hypothesis to measure).
  • Anonymized data on when and where peaks happen.

Water and the country

  • Every leak caught early is water left in Tunisia's dams.
  • Less electricity at peak means less gas burned.
  • Cheap parts (ESP32, PZEM-004T, YF-S201): affordable at scale.

What we say honestly to the jury

  • The savings assume the tranche price applies to every kWh of the month; to confirm on a real STEG bill.
  • Water saving assumes 12 m³ a month of normal use; SONEDE bands are per quarter and scaled to a month.
  • Studies report 5 to 15 % less consumption with real-time feedback: an order of magnitude to source.
  • The box measures within about 2 to 5 %: an estimate tool, not a billing meter. The family and the pilot are illustrations.

"Cartheco doesn't ask families to live in the dark. It shows them the one kWh that costs 9.74 dinars, before they use it."
