The 3D tour shows where the sensors go. This continues the story: a family lives with Tranche Guard for a month, and we measure what changes.

The family

Meet the Ben Salah home

A family of four near Tunis. Subscribed power 6 kVA, an electric water heater, one air conditioner, a classic SONEDE meter. Like most Tunisian homes, they only see their consumption when the bill arrives, too late to act. (Fictional family; every number below is computed with the app's own tariff engine and labelled as an estimate.)

Act 1

Installation (your 3D tour, 0:00 to 0:57)

A clamp around the phase wire, a flow sensor after the SONEDE meter, one ESP32 box. Nothing touched on the STEG or SONEDE side. The box starts posting a reading every 5 seconds. The month already stands at 182 kWh.

Act 2

Living with Tranche Guard

  1. Day 1 · 19:40

    The evening peak

    The air conditioner and the oven run together. Power jumps to 5.6 kW, close to the 6 kVA limit. The month climbs to 191 kWh.

    Phone alert"Encore 9 kWh avant la tranche suivante (200 kWh). La franchir coûterait environ 9,23 DT de plus sur ta facture du mois (estimation)."

    What they do: open the tip, delay the oven to tomorrow lunch, set the AC to 26 °C.

  2. Day 2 · 03:00

    The water that never stops

    The toilet flapper leaks: 0.35 L/min, silent, all night. The flow never drops to zero between 01:00 and 03:00.

    Phone alert"Fuite probable : l'eau coule sans arrêt depuis 01:00 (2 h 00), environ 42 L utilisés. Vérifie la chasse d'eau et les robinets."

    What they do: replace the flapper the next morning (a few dinars at the hardware store). Without the alert, this leak wastes 504 L a day, about 15 m³ a month, and would only show on the SONEDE bill months later.

  3. Month 2 · day 10

    The forecast warns early

    New month. At their usual 7.2 kWh a day, the forecast says the month will end at 216 kWh, over the 200 kWh tranche.

    Phone alert"Au rythme actuel, tu vas dépasser 200 kWh ce mois-ci (prévision : 216 kWh). Passer cette tranche coûterait environ 9,23 DT de plus (estimation)."
    Tip"Pour rester sous 200 kWh, réduis d'environ 18 kWh d'ici la fin du mois (≈ 0,9 kWh par jour). Économie estimée : 13,28 DT."

    What they do: put the water heater on a timer (heat once, at night), unplug the TV box and chargers at night. About 0.9 kWh less per day, without discomfort.

  4. Month 2 · every day

    The game keeps them going

    The daily score stays above 80, the weekly challenge (5 % less than last week) is won, and two badges unlock: Journée sobre and Zéro fuite. The kids check the score on the phone.

  5. Month 2 · day 30

    The bill

    The month closes at 198 kWh, still in tranche 1. Estimated bill 43.47 DT instead of 56.74 DT on the old path.

    Score screen"Mois terminé sous 200 kWh : environ 13,28 DT économisés (estimation)."
Act 3

The impact

−13.28 DTon month 2's bill−23 % of the bill for −8 % of the kWh
−51.34 DTin a summer month with ACforecast 312 kWh, kept at 296: only −5 % kWh

Why so much money for so few kWh? Because of the tranche jump: crossing 200 kWh by one kWh adds 9.23 DT, crossing 300 kWh adds 46.02 DT (price and VAT both rise). Tranche Guard's job is to see the jump coming early enough to avoid it with small changes.

For the household

  • The bill becomes visible and predictable, every day, in dinars.
  • About 180 DT a year for a home that sits near a threshold (6 months like month 2 and 2 summer months, an illustration, not a promise).
  • Leaks fixed in hours instead of months.
  • Never punitive: the score has a floor and the goals are realistic.

For STEG and the grid

  • Homes shift the water heater out of the evening peak: about 2 kW per home at the worst hour.
  • Pilot of 1,000 homes: up to 2 MW less on the evening peak if they all shift (hypothesis to measure).
  • Anonymized, aggregated data on when peaks happen, street by street.
  • Next step: "peak day" events where opted-in homes delay big loads together.

For SONEDE and water

  • One toilet leak = 504 L a day. Found the first night instead of at the next bill.
  • Water is scarce in Tunisia: every leak fixed early is water left in the dams.
  • Water tranche alerts switch on as soon as the SONEDE grid is entered.

For the country

  • Less electricity at the peak means less gas burned to produce it.
  • Built from cheap parts (ESP32, PZEM-004T, YF-S201) and open software: affordable for Tunisian homes.
  • The same platform could later read STEG smart meters instead of our box.

What we say honestly to the jury

  • The savings above assume the tranche price applies to every kWh of the month (blueprint formula). If STEG prices progressively, month 2 saves 4.29 DT, not 13.28: we check this on a real bill.
  • Real-time feedback studies report 5 to 15 % less consumption: an order of magnitude to source, not a promise.
  • The box measures within about 2 to 5 %. It is an estimate tool, not a billing meter.
  • The family and the 1,000-home pilot are illustrations.
Closing line for the pitch

"Tranche Guard doesn't ask Tunisian families to consume much less. It shows them the 1 kWh that costs 9 dinars, before they use it."
